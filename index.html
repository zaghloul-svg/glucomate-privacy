GlucoMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026  ·  Last updated: November 2026

GlucoMate ("the app") is a personal glucose-tracking app for Android. Your health data is yours. This policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with your information.

1. What data the app collects

GlucoMate collects only the information you enter directly:

  • Glucose readings (value, timestamp, notes)
  • Meal records (description, optional photo, timestamp)
  • Health profile you configure (e.g. target glucose range, diabetes type)

No account, registration, or login is required.

2. Where your data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device. On-device only

GlucoMate uses an on-device SQLite database (via Android Room). No data is synced to a server or cloud storage operated by GlucoMate.

3. When data leaves your device

GlucoMate includes an optional AI-insights feature powered by the Anthropic API. Network request

When you use the AI analysis feature, the app sends a summary of your recent glucose context and/or meal photos to the Anthropic API solely to generate your personalised insight. This request is:

  • Made only when you explicitly tap the AI-insight action — not in the background.
  • Processed by Anthropic under Anthropic's Privacy Policy. GlucoMate does not store or further process the response beyond displaying it to you.

No other network requests are made by GlucoMate.

4. What is never collected

  • No backend server or database operated by GlucoMate
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporter or diagnostics service
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data shared with third parties (beyond the optional Anthropic API call you initiate)

5. Your rights — data export and deletion

Export: You can export all your data at any time from within the app. The app supports both JSON and CSV export formats.

Deletion: To delete all your data, go to Settings → Data → Delete all data inside the app. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device. Because GlucoMate has no server, uninstalling the app is a complete and permanent deletion — no data remains on any server operated by GlucoMate.

6. Children's privacy

GlucoMate is intended for adults managing their own glucose levels. We do not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated and, where required, users will be notified through the app.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Please open an issue in the glucomate-privacy repository or contact the developer directly via GitHub.
